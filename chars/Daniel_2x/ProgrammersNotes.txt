=======>MUGEN Daniel Notes<=======

Well, where do I start... When I first began my Daniel MUGEN character project, I didn't know what I was really getting into. It ended up as a huge mess for the first
release tbh. Well, I will tell you what actually went through my mind.

Well let's see here: We go back to March of Two Thousand nine! I always felt like, a feeling of putting my character in almost anything, AND I AM NOT KIDDING,
I always wanted to give something (be it a game, a picture, etc.) a touch of Daniel or Dan himself. And when I got to MUGEN, I was like "I GOTTA MAKE HIM HERE,"
as I didn't have anyone to make him (I actually asked one of my friends) I decided to take the grueling task BY MY OWN MEANS. GOD, I just read the MUGEN help files
SLIGHTLY. And I repeat, SLIGHTLY. An error that actually costed me A LOT.

So yeah, with underdeveloped artwork skills, Bare coding experience (thankfully I knew some basic coding like expressions and stuff) and a willing to make a character
I started the Daniel project. Little I did know it was gonna last MORE THAN TWO YEARS!!

So yeah, so went the first month of the making, yay, sprites were being made and some moves were replaced. One false fact though, is that he was a TOTAL SWAP of a
character, well I was first editing KFM when I first made the char... In fact, I have always used the same base char, just have organized it better, deleted anims, etc.
but it actually was either: A bad definition of a Spriteswap, OR, just trying to piss me off XD...... yeah it succeeded ¬¬

Yeah the immaturity is stunning at this part. Of how I became blind and egotistical thinking my char was great, you know, everything from here, yes, it really seems
wrong and I admit it. It was a FATAL wrong move, and I actually had some luck here, cause normally these first impressions stay forever and are true to A LOT OF PEOPLE
and, well they are still true to some people and I don't blame them; it's difficult to change first impressions anyway; but I'm glad some people got over that and I'm
even friends to one of these people. (You know who you are, you deserve a real pat in the back from me ;) )

Anyways, as time went by... I decided to give the sprites of Daniel an upgrade... so it went, two shades of green, one shade of skin and etc. This is the spriting
that was kept until mid-2011. While it wasn't the biggest deal (graphical updates should be the last ones), it was a step forward! (Yay!) But here you can see why I
did it, I tried to impress someone... Little did I know it wouldn't mean two cents... And that I should make updates for myself... But lots of people complained about
how he looked... so I did attempt to fix that. d:

A couple of months passed alongside life... my friend Stickma14 (which we haven't seen since a loooong time... whatever happenned to him, I hope it's not bad and maybe
he just got bored of stuff) helped me along to make my creations better. Oh man.. I swear to god I should have been less obstinated into changing stuff at first...
But luckily that got to work on the go. And after a little time he edited Daniel... Creating CvS Daniel. The first version to seemingly have fixed most of its issues.
I was impressed... Finally Daniel had been fixed good and from CvS Daniel... I got cues to continue forward into this character!

At this point. The quantity of additions and remotions had been huge, REALLY HUGE... I mean, several attacks removed, added, winposes in, winposes out GODDAMMIT!!
It's been so much I don't even remember! XD To think most of it has been gone... Really weird huh? But hey, it's like in games. While they are being developed they have
no choice to remove some stuff because of time constrains OR because its execution was awkward. THAT'S WHY NEW DANIEL HAS FEW SUPERS FROM HIS OLDER VERSIONS. They
simply won't do it anymore... (I cannot imagine Daniel doing the punch smash.... FWAK it would be weird...)

So there also was some MUGEN free time I took personally myself... It has always been like that.. sometimes I just like to work.. some other times I just don't.
Afterall this is a hobby... I don't get paid for making Daniel. But anyway, that free time I took... It was more of a block... and afterwards my old PC died (It was
like in April 2010 and it's THE REASON BEHIND THE LOSS OF PLENTY OF CHARACTERS). So when I got my laptop on my birthday (06/06/1994) I was really happy and I wanted to
do everything I had done back then! Even MUGEN! So I downloaded everything again and set things up! :D

So I made new sprites for Daniel after a little while and kept working again.. this is 2010 indeed! And I added even more moves... this version of Daniel never saw the
light of day though. Although some people had gotten it. (NOT TELLING WHO... I know you're looking for it <_<) So it was a secret... afterall anyways... I never really
used him much in videos. (hurr) So it was all cool!

But after a while it started to get old... I just didn't feel like creating because I couldn't get some stuff right... You could say it's really a motivational hobby..
if you do not feel like it you won't do it... Was this the end of Daniel? Yes... :/

BUT LOL, on 2011 I got the idea of making a Hi-res version! And so I began making it in flash.. but it was still the old design... after I drew that picture it began
to rise the idea of making a new permanent design of Daniel. Which turned out well if you ask me. It has hair :B

But I knew making it that way would have to require a rearrangement... so I sat down and chose what would make it well for him.. Some moves stood still
(Kick Smash -> Dizzy Dance, The steamroller, Green Rage Takeover -> Triple Spin Barrage) As well as changing the commands for making it easier.. and the comboing is
smoother and it actually works now! Most of these crazy and random moves had to go though... they really didn't fit for him....

What does explain the Touhouness in this character? Simply. The creator likes Touhou a lot, so he added his favorite characters in. While they are random moves. I
didn't nearly even care... People ask me why Daniel shoves Koakuma violently if I like Koakuma a lot, or why does he knock down Daniel, Flandre and Koakuma in that
intro.. or why does he use Koakuma as a bat, or why does Kasdeya (A Cheap Koakuma edit by Kasenfanboy24000 and PandaciaLake. THE BEST KOA EDIT EVER) appear? SIMPLY,
because there is no rule to not make it happen, this is MUGEN, thus, I can do whatever comes to my mind of I wish. XD

Also you can notice Daniel is less voice based now. And that's fine for me. It's not that Daniel is silent (He's actually very talkative), it's just I don't happen to
feel like voice acting... plus I love how much SFX based he is now d: (his SND file really has grown). He has songs for his intros, his voices are really randomized,
different effects for a lot of situations. I love it.

While I do think I have done great on this. I do think he could have used more slower attacks, Daniel can really be fast at times... but I'm not sure.. So far noone
has said he's too fast.. so I don't know! :3

And of course. There's is no creation without inspiration.. Main inspirational characters for me have been Homer Simpson (Early 2010), Pacman Delta (Early 2009)
Dee Bee Kaw (Present) (The crazyness does it, I love random characters), Several of my friends (The creators of Kasdeya and darkaz2), and Lucas (Present)

Well now all that's left is.... THROW THE PARTY MAN!111
