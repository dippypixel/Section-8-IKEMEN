---=INTRODUCTION=------------

Rigby is a slobbish, diminutive raccoon who works with his blue jay friend, Mordecai, as
groundskeepers on a golf course. They constantly vie for payment under their surly boss,
Benson the gumball machine. However, their day is never dull, as they are almost always
facing mystical forces or Eldritch abominations.

Rigby summons regular characters Skips and High Five Ghost. He plays slappy in one of his
winposes to a jaunty marching tune. This is an allusion to "The SpongeBob SquarePants Movie". 
In one of his intros, his holographic self from episode "The Best Burger in the World" disintegrates, 
allowing him to float down like Tanooki Mario in Super Mario Bros. 3. And then I guess I modeled 
his bugling after DDR's Marvin the Martian. Oh, and Marvel character Rocket Raccoon, whose moves
Rigby swipes from Ultimate Marvel vs. Capcom 3, appears to cheer him on.

Mordecai and Rigby have been left on hold for a very long amount of time by a company, and
begin to get irritated. They are not slow to vow that they will kick the butt of the caller for
every hour of their lives they have lost listening to the phone.
