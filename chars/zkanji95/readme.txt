Zombie Kanji
by DrKelexo

=====	KEY 	=====

X	-	Light Attack
Y	-	Medium Attack
Z	-	Heavy Attack
A	-	Special
B	-	Punk Cancel

=====	COMMAND BASICS	=====

> f + X
An overhead headbutt.

> f + Y
A lengthy step-in attack.

> f + Z
A mid-range chair slam. Causes ground bounce against aireal opponents, as well as on counter hit.

df + Z
> A mid-range sweep. Can be held to increase distance.

d + Z (In Air)
> A downward-swinging chair attack. Causes ground bounce on counter.

=====	BASIC THROWS	=====

> f + Z (Close)
> b + Z (Close)

=====	SPECIALS	=====

> b, d, f + X/Y/Z
A mid-range projectile. Can block other projectiles.

> f, d, df + X/Y/Z
A gap-closing leap attack. EX version has startup invincibility.

> d, d + X/Y/Z
A temporary self-buff. X grants damage at the cost of defense. Y grants defense at the cost of damage. Z grants an increase in Punk Meter gain at the cost of damage. EX version grants all positive benefits.

> d, b + X/Y/Z (Air OK)
A leaping, blockable throw attack. After the last hit, Kanji can cancel into his self-buff special, or a super move.

> A (Air OK)
A super armor stance. Can block low attacks by crouching. Damage taken is reduced, and the Punk Meter increases relative to the blocked move's damage.

> f + A (Air OK)
A special throw. Holding up, down or neither and pressing any attack button will toss the opponent in different ways. Left or right can be used to change the direction you toss them.

> d, b + A
An armored throw. After a short period of standing and tanking damage, he'll preform the f + A throw.

> B
A cancel that requires at least one full bar of Punk. After an attack lands, press B to reset Kanji, allowing him to start a new combo. Holding different directions allows Kanji to dash forward, jump upward, or stand still.

> A + B
Enters a state of enhanced Punkrockerdom. Can only be used if the meter is filled up twice. While the meter is blinking, all Punk Cancels are significantly reduced in cost. The meter drains during this time, and will empty if the round ends while this mode is active.

=====	SUPERS	=====

> QCF, QCF + X/Y/Z
Kanji yells loudly, blasting away anyone nearby. A fast, close-range blow.

> QCB, QCB + X/Y/Z
Kanji throws his chair across the screen at an incredible speed. The chair eats other projectiles.

> QCF, HCB + X/Y/Z
Kanji dashes forward, performing a combo on his opponent on contact.
