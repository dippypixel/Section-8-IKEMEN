Sandbert
Made by Somebody407

Made as a side project since I wanted to take a break on working on Mr. Game & Watch.

==Gameplay==
Sandbert is a 6-buttoned character who uses 4 buttons for attacks. Z is used to activate Roman Cancels, allowing Sandbert to reset into his idle state during attacks. C is used for dodging.
Sandbert utilizes many mechanics from different fighting games, with some examples being the Guilty Gear series and Super Smash Bros. Ultimate.

==Pros+Cons==
+ Insane comboability
+ Double jump + Wall jump

= High gravity

- Most attacks are generally unsafe on block
- Below-average damage output

==Mechanics==

Press F or B near a wall = Walljump

Press F or B twice / x+y = Dash/Run
                    F or B when dashing - Dashdance

Tap F/D = Parry

z or 3 attack buttons = Roman Cancel

c = Dodge

D, U / U after using Launcher / a+b = Super Jump

any 2 buttons when falling = Recovery

==Command Normals==

a/b while dashing forward = Dash Attack

==Specials==

QCF, x/y = Kiss - A straight-forward projectile that does very little damage, but has little to no commitment for using. A really annoying tool for opponents to deal with from afar, but refrain from using it close-range because of its low hitstun.

QCF, a/b = Sandbert Specter - Sandbert dashes forward at an incredibly fast speed, launching opponents upwards if hit. The opponent will be stuck in hitstun for a long time, allowing the move to be used as a combo starter/extender.
                              Does little damage and leaves Sandbert very unsafe if blocked. Press one of the kick buttons to cancel the move earlier.
			      Gives Sandbert projectile immunity, making it a good option for closing gaps between the opponent.

QCB, a/b = Shine - Sandbert flashes a blue aura around him, damaging nearby opponents. When used for the first time in a combo or in neutral, the opponent will flash blue and be in hitstun for much longer, allowing the move to be used as a combo starter/extender.
                   The move is also deceptively fast, coming out at frame 2! Does very little damage, however. Can be jump cancelled if not blocked.

DP, a/b = Demon Form - Sandbert's eyes start flashing red and gains the ability to fly for a short period of time. Press any direction to move.
                       Pressing one of the kick buttons causes Sandbert to grow horns and wings, damaging any nearby opponent at a larger radius.
                       Press c to cancel the move.

==Supers==

QCF, x+y = Blastbert (LEVEL 1) - Sandbert shoots a giant laser out of his mouth, dealing a large amount of damage to opponents in front of him.
                                 Takes a while to come out, however.

QCB, x+y = Sand Flash (LEVEL 1) - Sandbert creates a giant explosion behind him, launching any opponent near him and dealing a lot of damage to them.
				  Sandbert is left incredibly vulnerable if blocked or whiffed, however.

HCF, a+b = Sandbergeddon (LEVEL 3) - A cinematic(?) super that functions as a combo finisher.
				     Not effected by damage scaling.
