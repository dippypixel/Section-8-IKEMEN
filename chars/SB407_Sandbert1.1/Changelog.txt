==V1.1 (??/??/24)==
Compared to G&W, most of Sandbert's changes in the 1.1 update are directed towards mechanics/visuals and not much else.
Sandbert's moveset is completely unchanged besides Sandbergeddon's cutscene being redone, but his comboability is toned down a bit due to the added OTG IPS.

*[General]       - A large amount of quality-of-life changes to make him more in-line with my more recent characters
*[General]       - Now utilizes Vans' projectile reflection system
*[Mechanics]	 - Can now recover by pressing c.
*[Mechanics]	 - Roman Cancels have been slightly nerfed; using it decreases power gain by 80% instead of 75%.
*[Mechanics]	 - Updated underground juggle and corner push codes from P.O.T.S' newest versions of their characters
*[Mechanics]     - OTG IPS modified
*[Mechanics]     - Reduced default command time from 30 to 15
*[Visual]	 - More FX/SFX have been added in certain places to make Sandbert more visually striking.
*[Visual]	 - Now uses Rivals of Aether hitsounds instead of the SSBU hitsounds.
*[Visual]	 - Completely redone the cutscene for Sandbergeddon to look more visually exciting.
*[Visual]	 - Added Dream Eater compatibility with my Gengar
*[Visual]        - Replaced Sand Flash explosion and Parry projectile effects
*[Visual]        - Fixed 12th palette having messed up colors when facing left (was only notable on Demon Form)
*[Visual]        - Jump FX now properly stays on the ground instead of slightly above.
*[Optimization]  - Removed some leftover code from Mr. Game & Watch
*[A.I]           - Now has an AI. Can be configured from No AI, Normal or Brutal

==v1.0b-c (09/28/22-09/30/22)==
In two consequent updates, Sandbert recieved some bugfixes and mechanic changes.

*[Mechanics]	 - You can now wavedash out of a Super Jump.
*[Mechanics]  	 - You can be thrown while in jumpsquat if you are in the middle of a wavedash.
*[Bugfix]	 - Throws now add to damage scaling.
*[Bugfix]	 - Fixed a crash relating to air dodges (thanks to XBrosion for letting me know)
