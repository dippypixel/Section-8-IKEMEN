Info:
dexter is a boy genius who has a secret laboratory, His intelligence makes him the brains of the family and he is one of the world's greatest inventors
having created countless weird and amazing machines and experiments. He is always working on new inventions and creations every day
but his sister Dee Dee is always breaking into his lab and destroying his work.
