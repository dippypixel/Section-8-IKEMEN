Butthead for Mugen by The_None, 27.11.2014

Once upon a time, Beavis and Butthead deviated from their usual Music Video watchings by watching a news report about a fighting tournament that seemingly anybody can join in and show off. After Beavis's went into the tournament encouraged by the possibility of beating up Michael Bolton, Butt-Head also jumped into the fray of kicking some ass. Including Beavis for not inviting him the first time.

Note:This char is not suited for simul mode

Button Layout:
x	=	LP
y	=	HP
a	=	LK
b	=	HK
z	=	CD
c	=	RL

Target Combos:
Close.HP->HP
Close.LK/Close.LP->LP->LP->LK->Crouch.HK
Close.LK/Close.LP->Crouch.LK>Crouch.HK
Close.HP->LP->LP->LK->Crouch.HK

(J.G.Diablo)LP->LK->HP->HK
(J.G.Diablo)Air.LP->Air.LK->Air.HK->Air.HP

Misc:
-Blowback Attack:				HP+HK, or CD
-Dodge:						LP+LK, or RL
-Guard Cancel Attack:				while guarding, HP+HK, or CD (needs 1 power stock)
-Guard Cancel Evasion:				while guarding, LP+LK, or RL (needs 1 power stock)

Command moves:
-Is This It:					forward, LP, LP
-Here Comes The Hammer:				forward, LK
-Beat The Bastards:				up-close, backward/forward, HP
-Lift Your Skinny Fists Like Antennas to Heaven:up-close, backward/forward, HK
-Bad Breath:					mid-air, up-close, backward/forward, HP/HK

Special Moves:
-Stranger Than Fishin:				QCB+LK/HK (can be also done mid-air)
-You Fucking People Make Me Sick:		QCF+LP/HP (can be also done mid-air)
	-> Charge:				Hold Punch
-Useful Idiot:					QCB+LP/HP
	-> Successfull counters can be canceled into any attack
-The Dead Angle Which It Continues Showing:	HCF+LK/HK
-Trampoline:					mid-air, LK+HK
	-> Can be jumped out of or canceled into a jump attack

Lv.1 Super Moves:
-Jean Gary Diablo:				QCF,QCF+LK/HK (can be also done mid-air)
	-> resets the damage dampener
	-> probides a boost to attack speed and comboability
-Let There Be Rock:				QCF,QCF+LP/HP
	-> button strength determines the position of the fan

Lv.2 Super Moves:
-Penis I See:					QCB,QCB+LK/HK

Lv.3 Super Moves:
-Here It Goes Again:				QCB,QCF+LK/HK

Notes:
-Most of the moves can only be juggled once into themselves
