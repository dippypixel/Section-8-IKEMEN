MUSASHI by MelvanaInChains
Origin: KuaiDa ChuanShuo - Street Fighter IV (NES)
VA: Nobutoshi Kanna

WHAT'S INCLUDED IN THIS CONVERSION?
- Sprites
- All of the original sound effects
- All of his moves

WHAT'S NOT INCLUDED FROM THE ORIGINAL GAME?
- A Conbon clone .def, because uh...that's kinda pointless. His palette's included though.
- The original VS portrait. I went with his artwork found on the actual cartridge. By the way, is it me or does he look EXACTLY
like Amiba in that artwork...?
- The close attacks, they weren't very useful. Also they had REALLY bad animations.
- The 'jumping forward kick', just used the forward kick animation for the general jump kick. I kinda don't like how the straight
jump kick from the original just reused the crouch kick animation.
- Any CVS effects. Because I feel like I need to state that at this point, even if it isn't from the original.

WHAT'S EXCLUSIVE TO THIS CONVERSION?
- Abel's voice from Battle Arena Toshinden 3, just to add more voice to the character.
- Custom supers. Half of which TOOOOTALLY not ripped from Abel.
- Custom winposes.
- Taunt.
- Custom intro.
- The really long bouncing attack from the original is, in the form of a special move, split up into two different moves.

BIOGRAPHY:
During the official fourth tournament (as in the real one, the most known one is a fake tournament organized by M. Bison, who
is just trying to milk the franchise as much as he can), Ranboo overcame all of his opponents and came to meet his last challenger.
This turned out to be a time-traveling (through the use of a time-travel briefcase) samurai from feudal times only known by the vague
alias of 'Musashi', who would immediately kill Ranboo and set out to conquer the modern world to build his army.

He's also in search of his twin and sworn enemy, Conbon, who mimicks his appearance down to a tee, albeit with different colors.
Musashi feels he cannot truly win unless he kills his brother as well.
