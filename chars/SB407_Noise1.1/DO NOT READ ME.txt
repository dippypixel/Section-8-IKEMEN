The Doise
Made by Somebody407

Noise was originally supposed to be a composite between his playable moveset and that of his boss moveset. 
However, that ended up leading to him originally having a very disjointed and messy moveset (and is a major factor as to why I slacked off on developing him for about..2 or so years).
I then had the idea to make two seperate movesets for Noise, one based on primarily his moveset as a playable character, and another based on his boss fight.
...as such, the Doise was made into a fully-fledged character! He sports a moveset based on not only his boss appearance, but also takes cues from the "Bring Your Own Class" DOOM mod.


==Gameplay==
The Doise is a 6-buttoned character who uses 4 buttons for attacks. Z is used to activate Roman Cancels, allowing The Doise to reset into his idle state during attacks. C is used for dodging.
The Doise utilizes many mechanics from different fighting games, with some examples being the Guilty Gear series and Super Smash Bros. Ultimate.

==Pros+Cons==
+ Great long-range/zoning options
+ Higher than average health
+ Very high damage per hit

- Lacks many of Noise's fast movement options
- Generally sluggish attacks, can make him struggle at close-range
- Many specials have drawbacks that can either cause Doise to take damage, be left vulnerable, or kill him outright.

==Mechanics==

Press F or B twice / x+y = Run
		    F or B during Mach 1 - Dashdance
                    F or B during Mach 2 onwards - Turnaround
		    D when running - Roll

z or 3 attack buttons = Roman Cancel

c = Dodge

Tap F/D or s = Parry

D, U / U after using Launcher / a+b = High Jump

any 2 buttons when falling = Recovery

==Command Normals==

D, a (Air) = Body Slam - A modified version of Peppino's Body Slam. Noise starts plummeting to the ground at high speeds, ending with a shockwave that hits nearby opponents. Unlike Peppino's Body Slam, Foise's horizontal momentum is not affected when the move is active. Landing hit is an OTG.

UF, b (Air) = The Boxing Glove - Doise uses his...foot, to punch enemies. Don't miss or you will fall on your ass.
								 High damage if it connects, but is left to be whacked easily if whiffed. Cannot be Roman Cancelled out of.

==Specials==

D, DF, F, x/y (Ground) = Rock - Doise magically pulls out a bomb and kicks it forward, which slowly jumps towards the opponent after touching the ground at first. Great for allowing Doise to safely close gaps and has enough hitstun to effectively start combos. Also an excellent okizeme option.

D, DF, F, x/y (Air) = Jetpack Dive - Can be steered to one of three directions. When he hits a wall, he will start spinning around the arena before eventually exploding.
			   Can be cancelled into Spin.
			   Pressing c will make him detonate early.
			   
x/y + a/b (Ground/Air) = Spin - The Doise spins around. Can be moved around a bit during the move and also has projectile immunity. However, the move has a noticeable amount of recovery frames. 

F, D, DF, a/b (Ground/Air) = Pogo Stick - Upon hitting the opponent, Doise is able to cancel this attack into any of his other specials, making this move a great combo tool.
	D, b = Fall
	c = Cancel

F, D, DF, x/y (Ground) = Floppy Chaingun - Pulls out the floppiest chaingun you've ever seen. Shoots explosive projectiles, but they can hurt you too. Don't use it at close-range.
										   Starts malfunctioning the more you use
