==1.0 (10/18/26) - 1.0.1 (10/19/26)==
Besides giving him a few very minor buffs, this update is meant to address an emergency bugfix that fixes a bizzare glitch with his Level 3's rush move when used in the air.

*[Buff] - Health slightly increased (800 -> 850)
*[Buff] - Has a bit of an easier time building up meter. The Jetpack Dive explosion in particular gives a VERY generous amount of meter now, giving you more reason to finish with it perhaps.
*[Buff] - 5y has a slightly less wide second hurtbox, giving it a little more priority.
*[Buff] - 2y has a little bit more vertical hitbox priority, making it a bit better as an anti-air.
*[Buff] - Gave his Punch Throw *slightly* less endlag so its a little more reliable as a combo starter.
*[Buff] - Sausage Chaingun generally does more damage
*[Buff] - Can now ACTUALLY cancel Jetpack Dive to Drill Dive, as the readme suggested. Sorry about that!
*[Bugfix] - Fixed a glitch where Noise would clip through the floor when using his Level 3's rush part in the air.
*[Bugfix] - Fixed debug flood during lose pose
*[Bugfix] - Can no longer RC out of Noise Crusher until after you land. Additionally, you CAN now RC out of it if the projectiles hit even if the base hit does not.
*[Other] - Properly added descriptions for his supers in the readme.txt, oops!
