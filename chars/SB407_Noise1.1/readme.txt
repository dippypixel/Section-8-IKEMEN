The Noise
Made by Somebody407

==Gameplay==
The Noise is a 6-buttoned character who uses 4 buttons for attacks. Z is used to activate Roman Cancels, allowing The Noise to reset into his idle state during attacks. C is used for dodging.
The Noise utilizes many mechanics from different fighting games, with some examples being the Guilty Gear series and Super Smash Bros. Ultimate.
The Noise also utilizes the Mach system from his source game, Pizza Tower. More info on that below

==Pros+Cons==
+ Exceptionally good mobility as well as additional mobility options such as wall jumping and crawling; allows The Noise to apply insane pressure onto opponents
+ Solid mid-range options
+ Noise Bomb; an extremely versatile tool that gives Noise excellent okizeme and pressure

- Low health
- Generally poor hitbox priority for the most part
- Many moves have noticeable ending lag; leaves The Noise vulnerable if moves do not connect or are blocked.

==Mechanics==

Press F or B twice / x+y = Run
		    F or B during Mach 1 - Dashdance
                    F or B during Mach 2 onwards - Turnaround
		    D when running - Roll

z or 3 attack buttons = Roman Cancel

c = Dodge

Tap F/D or s = Parry

D, U / U after using Launcher / a+b = High Jump

any 2 buttons when falling = Recovery

===Mach System===
The Noise has a unique mechanic that takes inspiration from his source game.
The Noise will start running at Mach 1, starting off slower. As he continues to run, he will gradually get faster and faster until he reaches Mach 4.
The Noise can use various moves such as Belly Slide and Dive to reach certain speeds much quicker.
When at Mach 4, The Noise will gain a hitbox that deals a ton of damage! However, it takes a really long time to get to Mach 4 anyway so it'll rarely be used in a serious fight.

==Additional Movement Options==
The Noise has a handful of specific movement options that most characters lack.

Press F or B twice / x+y (in air) = Air Dash

U (in air) = Air Jump 

F by a wall (in air) = Wall Jump 

D, b (Air) = Drill Dive

==Command Normals==

D, a (Air) = Body Slam - A modified version of Peppino's Body Slam. Noise starts plummeting to the ground at high speeds, ending with a shockwave that hits nearby opponents. Unlike Peppino's Body Slam, Noise's horizontal momentum is not affected when the move is active. Landing hit is an OTG.

U, y (Air) = Aggro Upper - A modified version of Peppino's Rising Uppercut. A combo extender that sends him disproportionally high.

==Specials==

D, DF, F, a/b (Ground) = Noise Bomb - Noise magically pulls out a bomb and kicks it forward, which slowly jumps towards the opponent after touching the ground at first. 
Great for allowing Noise to safely close gaps and has enough hitstun to effectively start combos. Also an excellent okizeme option.

x/y + a/b (Ground/Air) = Spin - The Noise spins around. Can be moved around a bit during the move and also has projectile immunity. However, the move has a noticeable amount of recovery frames. 
What is most notable about this move is how it corrolates his Noise's Mach system. When used while Noise is already in Mach 3 or higher, Noise will go way further and deal more damage than before. Noise will also retain his Mach stage when the move is finished. However, Noise loses the projectile immunity in this state, making it not always more ideal. Additionally, Noise will tumble if the attack is blocked, leaving him open to being punished.
Cannot be used during running turnaround state.

F, D, DF, a/b = Super Jump (Ground) - The Noise charges and can move around when holding the horizontal direction buttons.
									When the button is released, he will jump up into the air and ram opponents above him until he reaches the ceiling.
									Can be cancelled into Spin or Jetpack Dive.
									Causes a soft knockdown when uncharged, but causes a hard knockdown instead if charged.

D, DF, F, x/y (Air) = Jetpack Dive - Can be steered to one of three directions. When he hits a wall, he will start spinning around the arena before either landing on the ground or eventually exploding.
			   Can be cancelled into Spin or Drill Dive.
			   Pressing c will make him detonate early.


==Supers==

F, D, DF, a+b (Ground/Air) = Super Taunt - The Noise does a Super Taunt, damaging and stunning any nearby opponent. 
										   The move covers a lot of range, and is a great combo starter.

F, D, DF, x+y (Ground/Air) = Noise Crusher - Noise pulls out his namesake crusher, jumps high into the air and ALL the way back down, creating shockwaves on both sides.
											 The shockwaves make it a solid combo extender in the corner, though will not trigger if the move is blocked.
											 

D, DF, F, x+y (Ground) = Sausage Chaingun - Noise brings out a minigun and starts blasting sausages from afar.
											Naturally has excellent range, but has a specific quirk where the individual sausages deal less damage the farther they go.
											To deal optimal damage, use it up close by the corner.

D, DB, B, x+y (Ground) = Noise's Bombastic Breakdown (LEVEL 3) - A two-part super.
																 When used initially, The Noise will enter an install state, gaining new properties.
																 He can now instantly enter Mach 3 when running, Super Jump will now be fully charged, and most importantly, Noise Bomb will be enhanced.
																 Use this super again in this state, and Noise will rush towards the opponent and trigger a cinematic.
																 Make sure it actually lands though, as if it misses, Noise will actually lose this state!
																 The cinematic part is not affected by damage scaling.
