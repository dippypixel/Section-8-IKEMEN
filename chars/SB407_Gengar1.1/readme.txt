Gengar
Made by somebody407

Uses sprites from Pokemon Type: Wild with moveset being a mix between Type: Wild, Pokken and stuff from the main Pokemon series.

==Gameplay==
Gengar is a 6-buttoned character who uses 4 buttons for attacks. Z is used to activate Roman Cancels, allowing Gengar to reset into his idle state during attacks. C is used for dodging.
Gengar utilizes many mechanics from different fighting games, with some examples being the Guilty Gear series and Super Smash Bros. Ultimate.

==Pros+Cons==
+ Great mix-up and pressure potential due to having a large amount of overhead and low hitting attacks, as well as amazing movement overall
+ Excellent corner pressure due to having moves that bring the opponent into corners (Shadow Punch and Hypnosis)
+ Shadow Stealth; a teleport dash that gives Gengar invincibility frames and can be jump cancelled (i.frames carry over too), as well as functioning as a secondary dodge
+ Air dash

= Insanely floaty

- Low health
- Low meter gain
- Generally stubby normals
- Lacks an OTG

==Mechanics==

Press F or B twice / x+y = Shadow Stealth - Gengar's replacement for a run.
					    Invincibility does not carry over when used during a wavedash.

Tap F/D = Parry

z or 3 attack buttons = Roman Cancel

c = Dodge

D, U / U after using Launcher / a+b = Super Jump

any 2 buttons when falling = Recovery

==Command Normals==

F/B + y (when close) = Mega Drain - Gengar grabs the opponent and drains health and power from them.

F/B + b (when close) = Ominous Wind - Gengar launches the opponent to the air, causing them to get hit by a purple wind.

y after Paper Smack / 4y = Paper Followup - A follow-up attack for Gengar's Stand y, but can be used on its own.
					    Has a long-lasting hitbox, making it a viable anti-air.

F+x = Shadow Claw - An overhead swipe. Holding the x button will make Gengar postpone the attack or not even attack at all.

F+y = Poison Jab - Gengar thrusts forward with its hand appearing purple. While being fast, it is noticably unsafe on block.
		   It is worth noting that the move has a high amount of hitstun, making it a great combo tool.
		   Using it again in a combo will put Gengar in notable disadvantage, so be sure to remember when you used it in a combo. 
		   Can be cancelled into from grounded light normals, as well as Stand y.

D+b while in air = Multi-Lick - Multi-hit move that sends Gengar downwards. Can be canceled into from light aerial normals.
				Gengar turns when not facing the opponent when the move's hitbox comes out, making the move a potential cross-up tool.

==Specials==

QCF, P = Shadow Ball - Gengar creates a ball of dark energy and shoots it at the opponent. 
		       The projectile moves slowly, allowing Gengar to easily follow it and use it for combos or to apply pressure. 
		       Can be held to make the projectile go faster and deal more damage.

QCB, P = Counter   -  Gengar taunts the opponent. When the opponent attacks it during its initial animation, it will then use Teleport and whack the opponent with a hammer.
		      However, Gengar is put in a hefty amount of ending lag if the opponent does not hit it during its taunt animation.
		      At the end of the move, Gengar can either hold down to fall faster, or hold forward to air dash forward.

DP, P = Shadow Punch - Gengar creates a portal that makes a giant fist appear behind or in front of the enemy.
		       While slow and often won't work in neutral, the move is useful for causing the opponent to panic and/or reposition themselves.
		       Additionally, the move is a great combo extender as most moves put the opponent in enough hitstun to be comboed into Shadow Punch.
		       Applies hard wallbounce to the opponent the first time it hits them in a combo, also adding to it bring a great combo starter/extender.
		       Light version comes out faster and appears in front of the enemy, launching them backwards. Best to use when the opponent is near the corner.
		       Heavy version comes out much slower, but appears behind the enemy and launches them forwards (often towards Gengar which will allow for easier combo potential).

HCF, P = Hypnosis   - Gengar hypnotizes nearby opponents, then teleports behind them to attack them.
		      Its main use is allowing Gengar to switch sides with the opponent, potentially getting it away from the corner. Cannot be comboed into.
		      Also has an animation compatibility so have fun adding that to your characters ;)


DP, K = Sludge Bomb - The projectiles gain overhead properties and can hit downed opponents on their descent.
		      Can be used to create an unblockable setup when combined with 2y or some of Gengar's movement tools.

==Supers==

QCF, PP = Dream Eater (LEVEL 1) - Gengar will attempt to hypnotize any nearby opponent. If successful, the opponent will fall asleep.
				  Gengar will then eat the opponent's dream, causing them to take serious damage and heal Gengar in the process.
				  Great combo starter/extender, but can only be used once per combo. Best when used in the corner as it makes combos easier.
				  Uses the same animation compatibility as Hypnosis by the way

QCB, PP = Night Shade (LEVEL 1) - Gengar creates a pillar of dark energy in front of itself.
				  Extremely fast startup and is completely disjointed. An overall excellent anti-air super.

DP, KK = Sludge Wave (LEVEL 1) - Essentially a stronger version of Sludge Bomb. The trajectories of each projectile is randomized.
				 It's generally not the most consistent tool on ground, but is extremely useful in juggle combos.

HCB, KK = Shadow Drop (LEVEL 3) - A cinematic super taken directly from its Synergy Burst from Pokken. Not affected by damage scaling.
