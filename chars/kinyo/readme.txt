The Kinyo Roadshow Mascot By The_None

Note:This char is not suited for simul mode


Movelist:

==Command Moves==
-Super Leg:				F+Z
-Throw:					up close, forward/back+Z
-Wraparound:				A (doing this from block and attacks requires 1 power stock)
-Guard Cancel Attack:			while guarding, Z (requires 1 power stock)

==Special Moves==
-Hat Cannon:				QCF+X/Y/Z
-Tripod Blader:				DP+X/Y/Z
-Trolley Attack:			QCB+X/Y/Z
-Ougi Resshin Zan:			RDP+X/Y/Z
-Title attack:				mid-air, just below the title text, QCF+X/Y/Z

==Super Moves==
-(Lv.1)Projection:			QCB+X+Y
-(Lv.1)Gatling Gun:			QCF+X+Y
	- use up/down to aim the gun
-(Lv.3)Wide Screen:			X+Y+Z

==Other notes==
Normal moves cancelable into other normals:
-- Standing Close Light
-- Standing Far Light
-- Standing Close Medium
-- Crouching Light
-- Jump Light

Moves jump-cancelable when hitting the aerial opponent:
-- Standing Close Medium
-- Crouching Heavy
