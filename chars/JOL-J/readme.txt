==========================================================
JOL-J by DrKelexo
==========================================================

A conversion of a Knuckle Fighter character, played and originally coded by JOL-J himself, and modified by B&JA.

==========================================================
BASICS
==========================================================

= AB or X
Forward-moving dodge that doesn't pass through the opponent.

= BC or Y
Throw command. Hold backwards for a reversed throw.

= 4B
Gut punch command basic

= 6B
Overhead command basic with invulnerability to crouching attacks. Causes a floorbounce on counter hit and on airborne opponents.

==========================================================
SPECIALS
==========================================================

= 464A - "hey im fake ryo"
A fake-out rapid punch. Not an actual attack, but can be cancelled into a dodge.

= 464B - "hey im real ryo"
A rapid-punch attack. Causes a wallbounce on hit.

= 623A or 623B - "UPPACUT"
A flying uppercut. Invincible on startup. Can be dodge cancelled during the start-up.

= 214A - "big swingy"
A big swing that causes a knockdown. Causes a floorbounce on counter hit and on airborne opponents.

= 214B - "big punchy"
A chargeable punch. Distance and damage is increased the longer its charged, and will cause a wallbounce on counter hit or at maximum charge. Can be cancelled to standing by pressing another button while charging, or dodge cancelled during charge or during a wallbounce hit.

==========================================================
SUPERS
==========================================================

= 236BC or 236Y - "GRIM REAPAH"
Big scythe swing. Causes floorbounce.

= 214BC or 214Y - "SWORDO"
Spinning sword that hits multiple times.

= 623BC or 623Y - "AXE"
Rising uppercut with an axe.

= Hold 4>6BC or Hold 4>6Y - "ADRIIILL"
Mid-distance drill attack.

= 236AB or 236X - "BEAT EM UP, GUY"
Ranbu super.

= 641236AB or 641236X - "whoa its fuckin kaiser wave"
Projectile super with a slow startup.

= 214AB or 214X - "G-G-GURLS" (LEVEL 2)
Uppercut that is start-up invincible.
