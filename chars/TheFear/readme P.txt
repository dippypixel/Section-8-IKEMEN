The Fear
From Knuckle Fighter X/Alpha

Latest version: 1.0 (07/04/2025)

Character author: Sergio P


--------- Description -------

A Knuckle Fighter X/Alpha character conversion.

"The Fear" is a thug with a extremely sadistic personality, has violent skills, and is very stubborn when it comes to using weapons, no hesitation whatsoever.

I found this guy a pretty fun and decent live action character, I was simply destined to put him in MUGEN since other good Knuckle Fighter live action characters got converted too. A few days later, after discovering The Fear of course... I became unlazy and started working on that conversion!


--------- Move List ---------

Light Punch - X

Medium Kick - Y

Heavy Kick - B

Hard Caress - Forward + Y (air version included)

Throw - Hold Forward near opponent + B

Playing with Fire - A

Violence Combination - Forward or Back + A (Can follow-up 2 times when you keep doing the command)

Lagrange Point - Down + A

Japanese Fucking Weapon - Down + Back + A (Requires 1 power bar)

Cleaning Blaster - Down + Forward + A (Requires 1 power bar)

Extreme Fireworks - Forward + Down + Forward + A (Requires 2 power bars)
