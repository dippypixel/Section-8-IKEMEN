---Obligatory background story.-

Years after the SF2 Tournament, Ryu had achieved the title of 'Ultimate Master of the Fist' by beating Gouki in a fair match. As his master's brother disappeared into the shadows, Ryu felt a weird sensation.Sure, he was now the 'Ultimate Master of the Fist' and he was very happy about that, but a doubt crossed his mind. He had fought for all of his life in order to achieve that title. He knew ONLY how to fight. Nothing more, nothing less. So, how was he going to spend the rest of his life?
He started a dojo at first, but after a few months he had to shut it down due the lack of disciples, which were attracted by the easier to learn Saikyo-ryuu. Ryu could not stand the fact that his Ansatsuken was looked over for Dan's style, and fell in depression. Having nothing else to do, he went to Tokyo, met Sakura once more, and settled down with her.
Ryu, having no school degrees at all, joined at first some wrestling league, but left after he realized that it was all fake, then found a job at a McDonalds restaurant. McDonalds was like fighting to him: he devoted his whole life to serve the red and yellow clown, which earned to him a promotion of manager. Yet, his paycheck, along with Sakura's, was not enough to make a decent living. He fell further into depression and started drinking, while his wife secretly dated other men because of her husband's lack of sexual education (everytime she initiated foreplay, he took it as a threat, and Shoryuken'ed her out of bed. After she tried to tell him about the flower and bees, he ran away crying, so she decided not to tell him anymore...but that's another completely unrelated story).
Their relationship was falling apart, but he forced himself to believe that it was just a phase of their life, and that everything would be fixed someday. But then, he received the final insult.
Late at night, after his shift was over, he came back to his house holding an Happy Meal for Sakura, but she was nowhere to be found. When he went in their room, he suddendly listened to the girl doing something naughty with another man, another man whose voice haunted his nightmares.
'WHAT THE FUCK' he thought as he opened the door, realizing that Sakura was doing 'something' with Gouki.
He ran out of the house crying as a baby without even listening to explanations and started living in a dump, asking for the Gods to give him another chance. Then, he found it: his old gi, the same gi that was thrown in the litter years before, was lying on the ground in front of him. He took it as a sign from the Gods, wore his gi, and started fighting again. After amonth of training 24/7, he was already back in good shape, but his control over his power, after years of enduring McDonald's meals and heavy drinking, was severely damaged. In order to regain his strength, he decided to hit the street again, fighting his way and, who knows, maybe finding Gouki and getting his REVEEEEEEEENGE on him.

But something was different that time: he was not Evil, not Dark, not LV2, not LV3, not SSJ, not Orochi, not Serious, not Malicious, not Drunk, not Niggah, not Violent, not God, not Shin, not Imperfect Shoto, not Accurate, not Spriteswapped, not The Crap Renamed Version You Download From Other Sites, not anything. 

He was just SLIGHTLY PISSED OFF at the world.

SPO Ryu's attacks, despite being ridicolous looking and apparently useless, are POWERFUL and can deal serious damage when comboed. Why is that? Despite Ryu not having control on his powers anymore, he STILL is the Ultimate Master of the Fist. Hence, his attacks are supposed to deal one hell of a damage.

-SPECIALS

 -EX- version of certain special attacks can be done if you hit two kick/punch buttons instead of one. EX attacks have various advantages over special attacks and deal more damage.
They require 300 power to be used, although they give some back when hitting.

YOU FAIL AT Hadoken
D,DF,F,p.
Ryu tries to do his Hadoken, but fails. Weak version is just a slap, medium and strong have him throw a spit of energy from his palms. Still, is painful as hell.
 -EX - Ryu shoots a burst of energy from his palms.

YOU FAIL AT Shakunetsu Hadoken
B,DB,D,DF,F,p.
Ryu tries to do his Shakunetsu Hadoken, but ends up shooting a weak burst of flame from his hands, which still sets the opponent on fire.
 -EX - Ryu shoots a flaming projectile which covers almost the full screen.

YOU FAIL AT Shoryuken
F,D,DF,p.
Ryu slides forward. Once you tap punch again, Ryu does his Shoryuken. He cannot control it fully, thoug.
The first punch determines how far Ryu goes with his dash, the second one determines the punch itself: weak version is just a spinning punch, medium makes him jump a little, strong makes him fly high, but he ends up landing on his butt.
 -EX - Ryu makes his Shoryuken and miracolously lands on his feet. 

Shoryuken Slip
k during the dash of Y.F.A. Shoryuken.
Ryu slips during his Shoryuken, taking the opponent along with himself!

YOU FAIL AT TatsumakiSenpuuKyaku
D,DB,B,k.
Ryu makes his TatsumakiSenpuuKyaku, but goes BACKWARDS. Kick strength determines how much backward he goes and how many hits.
If done into the air, Ryu will spin a bit, then fall.
 -EX - 
Ground: Ryu makes his TatsumakiSenpuuKyaku, and miracolously goes forward.
Air: Ryu will use the true power of the TatsumakiSenpuuKyaku and spin around as fast as he can!

THE ELITE BUTT THROW
F,D,DF,k.
Ryu lunges at the opponent, trying to catch them. Should he succeed, he will use the legendary attack that Sheng Long used before him to throw Vega out of the screen in SF2: the ELITE BUTT THROW, aka a throw from behind. You can't combo into this move though.
-EX - Ryu is invincible for the whole duration of the move. Use and abuse.

Lunch Rush Attack
F,D,B,p.
Is your opponent hungry? What's better than  grab something outside the screen to quench his hunger, or, at least, to shut the hell up of him? 
Ryu will throw a McDonalds Meal at his foe, hoping that he will be distracted long enough eating so that he can hit him. Too bad, he doesn't realize how much damaging the meal is...
-EX- Ryu summons the corpse of Vega (the caped guy, in case you're wondering) and swiftly throws it at the opponent! 




-SUPERS
Ryu's super work a little differently from normal supers: each 'normal' super has 3 different versions, which come out at random. The higher the button strength you use, the % of occurrancy of 'negative' versions goes down, but the higher the backfire effect if they do.

YOU FAIL AT Shinkuu Hadoken
D,DF,F,D,DF,F,p.
 Ryu tries to do his Shinkuu Hadoken. Various things can happen.
-Ryu shoots a burst of energy from his hands.
-Ryu shoots a fireball that travels for a bit, then turn backs and hits him in the face, causing non-lethal damage to him. 
-Ryu shoot a fireball that HEALS the opponent for a little bit for life, but causes great chip damage when blocked.


YOU FAIL AT Shinkuu TatsumakiSenpuuKyaku
D,DB,B,D,DB,B,k.
Three things may happen.
-Ryu actually succeeds in using his TatsumakiSenpuuKyaku! Too bad his body can't stand it anymore due to the lack of training, and he ends up breaking his ankle while landing.
-Ryu actually succeeds in using his TatsumakiSenpuuKyaku! Too bad he loses control halfway through and flies around the screen, falling on the ground afterwards.
-Ryu actually succeeds in using his TatsumakiSenpuuKyaku! Too bad he loses control immediately and starts spinning so fast that eventually he's thrown out of it and crashes into the wall with a BIG explosion, causing great damage if not blocked.


YOU FAIL AT Shin Shoryuken (LV3 power)
D,DF,F,D,DF,F,k.
Ryu tries his fatal ultimate attack, only to have it explode in his face when he goes for the final punch.
Deals 90% damage to both players. If the first hit misses, Ryu does a stronger Shoryuken which ends with him falling on his butt and causing a shockwave.


ULTRA PISSED OFF mode (LV3 power)
x,x,F,a,z
Ryu gets EXTRA PISSED POWER. The moment you enter this mode, your power will start decreasing quickly until you hit 0, which reverts you to Slightly Pissed Off mode.
Once you're out, it takes a bit for Ryu to cool down, so be careful.
-He gets a slight attack boost.
-Using any of his special moves will result in the EX version coming out for free.
-You can freely use supers during this period - although using them will drain your bar faster, shortening your time in UPO mode.
-When hit, he will recover power fast. The longer the combo, the more pissed he'll get, the more power he'll get. BEWARE.
-His taunt will cause him to SHOW THE WORLD HIS ANGER.
You can charge your power bar to avoid getting 0 power. 
Note that Ryu will automatically enter in permanent UPO mode against certain characters, and in normal UPO mode against others.
