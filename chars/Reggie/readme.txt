REGGIE SKATORE by MelvanaInChains (version 2)
PORTRAYED/VOICED by Reggie Dyson

BIOGRAPHY:
A former friend of MDI's, Reggie Dyson used to be a sensible and oveerall normal person. One day, trying to get a Baconator at Wendy's, he was attacked by a bizarre looking man who identified himself as Count Crackula. In the scuffle, Reggie was knocked unconscious due to Crackula striking him with the Stand Arrow. He gained a Stand in the process, but his mental state was completely changed to that of a cunning and calculating criminal mastermind, and is now an expert at free-style Kendo despite only using a stick of wood. The stick of wood, however, is a mysterious and dangerous type of material that is produced by his Stand.
Reggie Dyson now knows himself as Reggie Skatore in this new form.

STAND (PORTRAYED/VOICED by Syed Jamal):
Disco Volante is able to allow him to generate wood at impossible speeds, and the wood itself is incredibly strong upon impact. Reggie is able to build complex constructs with it in a matter of seconds using it, even with ordinary wood.
Those who aren't Reggie that try and retrieve the Stand's wood for themselves will begin to disintegrate from the inside upon contact.

To Reggie's disturbance, Disco Volante's actual visible form inexplicably looks exactly like Count Crackula.

BUTTONS:
X/Y - PUNCH
A/B - KICK
Z - HEAVY ATTACK
C - DODGE

SPECIALS:
* Pavement Saw (charge back, forward, P):
Weak variant dashes forward a bit with a swift slash from his staff.
Heavy variant dashes further and hits three times, but has /considerably longer start-up.

* Natural High of Bloodstone (charge down, up, P)
Weak variant shoots Reggie upward for a short distance and height.
Heavy variant has longer start-up but shoots farther upward, takes him a bit farther and hits three times.

* Bye Bye Bye (charge back, forward, K)
Weak variant dashes forward a bit with a sliding kick that hits low.
Heavy variant dashes further and hits three times, but has considerably longer start-up.

* The Miracle of Sound in Motion [air only] (qcb, LK)
Rising upward kick. Lasts longer on the heavy variant.

SUPERS:
* Good Weather for an Airstrike (qcf, qcf, P)
Basically very reminiscent of DIO's knife super, hurls a barrage of multiple objects such as wooden spikes, Wendy's Frosties, etc. Air variant shoots these downward.

* Crash Today (qcb, qcf, K)
Dashes forward with an elbow strike, and upon striking the opponent, he follows it up with an additional slash. If missed or guarded, he is open for a retort.

** Didn't We Deserve a Look at You the Way You Really Are (qcf, qcf, K) 
Basically a take off of Hyo Imawano's super; Reggie throws out a kick. Upno connecting, a wooden clone of Reggie appears behind the opponent and they begin pummeling the opponent and end with a variant of Natural High of Bloodstone. If it misses Reggie has no cooldown and isn't open for an attack (again like Hyo's).

*** Would? (qcb, qcf, P)
Reggie dashes forward very far and very fast, and upon connecting, Volante turns the opponent into a wooden statue of themselves. Reggie then dashes back and forth in front of them very fast, then slowly turns around as the delayed slashes occur and then break the statue, launching the opponent out of it.
