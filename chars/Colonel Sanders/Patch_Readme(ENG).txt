=====<How to use & Caution>=====

 - Overwrite put all the files in the folder "Colonel".
 - It is recommended that you always have a backup.


=====<Movelist>=====

 U - up          x - light punch        a - light kick
 D - down        y - heavy punch        b - heavy kick
 F - forward     z - system move 1      c - system move 2
 B - back        p - any punch          k - any kick
 s - start       2p- two punches        2k- two kicks
 
 (Air) - Move must be performed in the air.


<THROW / UNIQUE MOVES>

.Throw                              F/B + 2P  (near opponent)


<SPECIAL MOVES>

.Drum                               D, DF, F, p
.Wing                               F, D, DF, p         (Can attack even after Drum hit or guarded)
.Rib                                D, DF, F, p  (Air)  (Can attack even after Wing)
.Thigh                              D, DF, F, k  (Air)  (Can attack even after Wing)
.Keel(Projectile)                   D, DF, F, k  (hold to delay)
.Keel(Barrier)                      D, DB, B, p


<RAGE MOVES>

.Wing                               F, D, DF, 2p


<SUPER MOVES>

 <LEVEL 1>

.Iai                                D, DF, F, D, DF, F, p
.Twister                            D, DB, B, D, DB, B, k  (near opponent)
.Pressure Fryer                     D, DB, B, D, DB, B, p


 <LEVEL 2>

.Colonel 3 dozen                    D, DF, F, D, DF, F, k
  (In Japanese, "3" is pronounced "san", and "dozen" is pronounced "dars".)
.Raging Colonel                     D, DF, F, D, DF, F, 2p


 <LEVEL 3>

.The Whole Piece                    D, DB, B, D, DB, B, 2k



                    <<<SYSTEM>>>

.Run                                F, F
.Backward Run                       B, B
.High Jump                          tap D, U
.Forward Roll                       x + a  /  z
.Backward Roll                      B + x + a  /  B + z
.Parry High                         tap F
.Parry Low                          tap D
.Air Parry                          tap F                  (Air)
.Power Charge                       hold c  /  hold y + b
.Fall Recovery                      2p / 2k                (while falling and allowed)



=====<Gameplay Notes>=====

NORMAL:

 - It is a normal performance.


RAGE SYSTEM:

 [MERIT] <Effect appears when hit point is less than or equal to about 40%.>
 - Attack power is increased.
 - Power gauge is constantly rising.
 - Can use Rage Moves.

 [DEMERIT]
 - Defense is lowered.
