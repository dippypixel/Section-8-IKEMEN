-----------------------------------------

basic movements
---------------------
x - light attack
y - medium attack
z - hard attack
a - STAND MODE on/off

b - Roll (STAND OFF only)

(quick) U          - short jump
(in the air) U     - air jump (STAND ON only)
(while blocking) b - Advancing Guard
(STAND OFF) a + attack - Attack with STAND MODE on

special movements
---------------------
D,DF,F, x  - dash upper rush
D,DF,F, y  - zoom punch
 (while hitting) B  - draw the enemy
D,DB,B, y  - hidden punch (crouching attack)
D + x + y  - throw
D,D + x or y or z  - warp

hyper movements
---------------------
D,DF,F, xy  - Arrivederci!
D,DB,B, a   - Tandem Combo


-Tandem Combo-
It memorizes the command that you input by the end of a pause.
When after the pose end or you separate A button, it make a replay.

-STAND MODE on-
It is an aggressive mode. Many Attacks can link!
Life is not reduced during guard. 
But, Please be careful to STAND CRASH!

-AI Level-
Check the "_Config.txt" and change var(57). 
you can choose 5 or 25 or 100.
5 is strong, 25 is medium, and 100 is weak.

-To hide a Message Icon or STAND MODE GAUGE-
Check the "_Config.txt" and change var(15) or var(16). 
when you transfer "1" to "0", the gauge hides.
