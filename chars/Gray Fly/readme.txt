Gray Fly By Kakyoin-Kun

Orign: JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Heritage For The Future (About to be playable but cutted off as a unused character)


The features:

Warusaki3's JoJo HFTF Sprites 
No Stand Mode
Ripped Sprites From Development Mode
2 Voices, one HFTF and Anime (Diamond Records)


Key Commands:

x: Low Attack
y: Medium Attack
z: Strong Attack
a: Stand On and Stand Off (He don't have a stand bar, but pressing the stand button makes him fly to attack the enemy)
b: Dash Escape
c: Unbound
s: Taunt


Special Moves:

Rip it out!: 236 + x  236 + y  236 + z

Masscare!: 214 + x  214 + y  214 + z


Super Moves: 

Bingo!: 236 + x+y  236 + y+z  236 + x+z  236 + b

You Can't Get Me With Your Own Speed!: 214 + x+y  214 + y+z  214 + x+z  214 + b
