Blizzard From Primal Rage (with Chaos mode included) for Mugen by The_None

To install the character, extract the files into the directory "chars\blizzard\" of MUGEN and open select.cfg and.....
-Winmugen users, add the line:"blizzard, stages/[name of stage].def"
-1.0 users, add the line:"blizzard/blizzard10, stages/[name of stage].def"
To access the "Chaos Groove", you can either select Blizzard with a start+ pallete, or for those wishing for the accurate size as well....
-Winmugen users, add the line:"blizzard/chaos, stages/[name of stage].def"
-1.0 users, add the line:"blizzard/chaos10, stages/[name of stage].def"

Deperation attacks are availble only during the ballistic mode, which is obtained when knocked down, under 30% and under half of the opponent's health.

Blizzard Groove:

Blizzard is a noble god, the essence of the animal spirit. Frozen for millenia at the heart of an immense glacier in the Himalayas, Blizzard was released when the Urth met the great meteor. He lived high in the mountains, descending only when threatened, and with his animal power and age-old wisdom, he's almost unstoppable.

Chaos Groove (select with start+):

Chaos was the mightiest witch doctor on the first continent. His greatest wish was to control the evolution of humanity. In his quest for power, he cast a mighty spell which backfired and turned him into a disgusting beast, forced to wallow in his own excrement for eons. During the cataclysm, Chaos prayed to Throshti, god of carrion, for release. However, to restore his greatness, he had to defeat all his enemies.
