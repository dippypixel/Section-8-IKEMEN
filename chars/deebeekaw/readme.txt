Gameplay notes:

-He has an unique juggle system that only allows you to juggle with moves you have yet to juggle into. (Supers are extempt from this rule)
-The juggle system treats far and close variants of a basic attack as the same move. However, ground and air variants of a special attack is treated as an separate attack.
-HADOOOwhat is randomized. There are about nine possible kinds of projectiles this move may spew out. You can reroll the selection via taping the button before he's about to throw the projectile.
-At times utilizing drive brakes can prove to be quite useful
-If you manage to hit with a ground Gen'ei Kyaku it's a good idea to start charging down for following up with Rising Grandpa (unless you have already used it in your current juggle combo)
-Trapping the opponent with a beartrap gives you decent oppoturnity to take pills
-It's not a good idea to take pills if the neurotic level isn't high enough (at the green level)
