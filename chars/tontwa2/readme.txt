Personality:
Tontwa is the kind of kid you knew in middle school who would randomly insert 
himself into your friend group, start quoting random, obscure YTP titles, 
and then would act confused when everyone looks at him weird.

He constantly chases whatever weird thing he currently thinks is funny. 
Whether it's a meme that died years ago, a random catchphrase, or some weird,
hyper-specific injoke to him and HIM only, Tontwa will repeat it endlessly 
until everyone around him is sick of hearing it. 

He has a habit of doing dumb things and then confidently insisting they're 
cool, such as punching people in the face while shouting "BANG!" at school, 
only to insist it's "the next big thing."

Despite being annoying and pretentious, Tontwa usually means well. He's 
friendly, awkward, and always looking for something fun to do. Unfortunately, 
his complete lack of self-awareness often causes him to become a nuisance 
without him realizing it.


===============================================================================
----------------------------------- OVERVIEW ----------------------------------
===============================================================================

Tontwa is a 6-button stance character that can alternate between a standard 
shoto playstyle, to a rushdown playstyle on the fly. Tontwa's kit is designed 
to be approachable and easy-to-pick-up for both beginners and experienced 
players alike.

===============================================================================
-------------------------------- MODE-SWITCHING -------------------------------
===============================================================================

As mentioned in the OVERVIEW section of this readme, Tontwa's main gimmick is 
his ability to swap between fighting styles on a whim. You're likely wondering 
what both of these playstyles are like, and how they affect Tontwa.

Allow me to break it down:

---------------------------------- SHOTO-MODE ---------------------------------

This is the standard playstyle Tontwa defaults to when you enter a match. His 
moves are generally more balanced and don't lean too hard into other archetypes. 
Things such as his damage and speed are tuned to be relatively balanced, while 
still being sufficient enough in a competitive match.

----------------------------------- RUSH-MODE ---------------------------------

This is the rushdown playstyle Tontwa can switch to during battle. Unlike 
Shoto-Mode, Tontwa's moves are far spammier and combo-heavy, but damage is 
lowered so that he doesn't effortlessly trample over the opponent within a 
matter of seconds.

-------------------------------- SPECIAL MOVES --------------------------------

Hadouken (Down, Forward, Light Punch)
Tontwa shoots a simple projectile. Slow and easy to evade. Deals a standard 
amount of damage.

The Walking Man (Down, Forward, Light Kick)
Tontwa will call for an assist to walk forward. When the assist touches you, 
you will be stunned; leaving you vulnerable for Tontwa to combo into you. 
Basically functions as a more OP version of the EX Hadouken, but unlike the 
projectile, you can easily counter the assist by simply hitting it.

Shove (Down, Back, Light Punch)
Tontwa does a basic shove, pushing the opponent away.

Bang (Down, Down, Back, Light Punch)
Tontwa charges forward with his fist while shouting "BANG!" This move is pretty
easy to avoid and deals minimal damage.

NOTE: ALL EX SPECIALS COST 500 POWER!!!
EX Hadouken (Down, Forward, Hard Punch)
Unlike the regular variant, this projectile combos the enemy as it passes 
through. This is an excellent combo-extender.

The EX Walking Man (Down, Forward, Hard Kick)
Similar to the aformentioned special, a faster variant will pass by, dealing 
much more damage.

Shove EX (Down, Back, Hard Punch)
Unlike the regular shove, Tontwa shoves the opponent 3 times, with the last
shove pushing the opponent further away. An excellen get-off-me tool for the
low, low price of 500 meter! In addition, a flood of Lego studs will spill 
out, crashing the Lego economy... or whatever.

Bang EX (Down, Down, Back, Hard Punch)
Instead of charging forward a minimal distance to do a singular punch, Tontwa
instead hits the opponent 4 times, with the final hit exploding the opponent
upwards; thus living up to the name of the move!

--------------------------------- SUPER MOVES ---------------------------------

SHOTO SUPERS:
**Junior Meal (Down, Back, Down, Back, Heavy Punch)**
Costs 1,000 Power

Tontwa moves towards the opponent, before dealing 4 devastating blows, finished
with a powerful kick.

This move is very easy to counter if you hit Tontwa before the move starts, 
however, or if he activates it too far away from the opponent.


**Combo Meal (Down, Back, Down, Back, Spin)**
Costs 2,000 Power

Tontwa performs a fast-paced, rapid 30-hit combo on the opponent, finishing it 
off with a final, devastating elbow thrust that launches them away. 

This move is easy to counter, much like the Junior Meal super.


**FOOT (Down, Back, Down, Back, Light Kick)**
Costs 3,000 Power

Tontwa attempts to perform an ultimate move, but ends up pissing off some giant 
in the sky; leading to said giant attempting to stomp on Tontwa. 

However, they miss and end up stomping on the opponent instead. This move 
cannot be countered or guarded unless the opponent is lying on the ground.


**ULTIMATE RUNNING OF THE BULLS (Down, Back, Down, Back, Strong Kick)**
Costs 3,000 Power

Tontwa charges forward and grabs the enemy. If missed, the move will end 
instantly, leaving Tontwa vulnerable for a brief period.

If grabbed successfully, the move is shortly interrupted by someone behind 
Tontwa shouting that the UROTB have officially begun, and they're charging 
forward to stampede. As such, Tontwa begins running away, and EVERYONE tramples
over the opponent(s), followed with a stampede of the UROTB, dealing MASSIVE 
damage. The move finishes off with a large BRAZEN BULL to storm through the 
opponent(s).



RUSH SUPERS:
Super Spin (Down, Back, Down, Back, Spin)
Costs at least 1,500 Power

Tonwa has a rare medical condition, where any time Dead or Alive's You Spin Me 
Round plays, he will start to spin out of control. 

Tontwa is controlled and accelerated to spin faster using the forward or back 
buttons. The longer you hold the button down, the faster Tontwa will move and 
the higher the combo will get. Depending on how much power you have, you'll do 
far more damage during the move; alongside the Ed Edd n Eddy Japanese sneeze 
sound playing at the end if the super was activated at full power.

At the end of the super, Tontwa delivers a devastating elbow jab; launching the 
enemy upwards.
