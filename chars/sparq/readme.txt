Sparq from Thea Realm Fighters By The_None

Note:This char is not suited for simul mode


Gameplay notes:
-Only moves you've already juggled with in a combo take off juggle points
-The moves that knock up grounded opponents cannot be juggled into themselves, you'd have to be more inventive than that

Movelist:

==Basic Attacks==
-Punch:					X
-Kick:					Y
-Knockdown:				Z

==Command Moves==
-Dodge:					A
-Electro Fist:				F+X
-Throw:					up close, forward+Z

==Special Moves==
-Power Fist:				QCF+X/Y/Z
-Electro Bolt:				QCB+X/Y/Z
-Thunder Call:				DP+X/Y/Z
-Horizontal Canonball:			Charge Back, F+X/Y/Z
-Vertical Canonball:			Charge Down, U+X/Y/Z
-Electro Penetrator:			F,F+X/Y/Z

==Super Moves==
-Kick of Power:				QCF+X+Y
-Hall of Thunder:			QCB+X+Y
-(LV.3)Elements of the Past		X+Y+Z
