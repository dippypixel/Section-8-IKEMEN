----MOVELIST/OMAKE----
Special Functions
-Holding up when dashing will allow you to fly upwards for a short time. 
-You can dash out of back dashing.
-You can attack from back dashes

Command Specials
-Goomba Stomp: D, a (Air Only, Comboable)
-Flying Kick: F, b (Air Dash only) 

Normal Specials
-Halito: D, DF, F, x/y
-Badios: D, DF, F, x/y (Air Only)
-Big Halito: F, DF, D, DB, B, x/y 
-Dalto: D, DF, F, a/b
-Molto: D, BD, B, a/b
-Dilto: D, D, a/b 
-Rider Kick: D, BD, B, a (Air Only)
-Katino: c
-Teleport: z (Can be used in air)
-Pursuit: U, x/y/a/b (When Opponent is downed) 
-Counter: x+y (When blocking with 1 bar of power)
-Dodge: a+b (When blocking with 1 bar of power)

Hypers Commands
----LEVEL 1----
-Firenado: D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x/y
-Super Dalto: D, DF, F, D, DF, F, a/b

----LEVEL 2----
-Giant Fist: D, DB, B, D, DB, B, x/y
-Final Flare: D, DB, B, D, DB, B, a/b
