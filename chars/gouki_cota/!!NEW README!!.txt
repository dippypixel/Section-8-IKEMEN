v1.0, 20/11/26 (DD/MM/YY)

Edit of Nobuyuki's conversion of Gouki from X-Men: Children of the Atom.
What started off as a simple AI patch spiralled into a full-on edit.

;------------
;INFO
;------------

Nobuyuki's Gouki is a very impressive conversion for its time period, but it isn't perfect.
While working on the patch, I slowly fixed inaccuracies (mentioned in the "What's Changed?" section) and it got to a point where I'd comfortably consider it an edit.
As stated earlier, it is also an AI patch. Be warned that it plays like a TAS, and isn't meant for human play! (Unless you feel daring, in that case go right ahead.)

Oh, and a 12P mode was added that removes the damage dampener for gits and shiggles.


;------------
;WHAT'S CHANGED?
;------------

- AI added, obviously.
- Portrait added (albeit, just taken from felineki's ST Akuma. XCOTA doesn't have any portraits.)
- Scaling has been changed to match how XCOTA would look in 4:3 aspect ratio.
- Intro and win poses have been changed to be more accurate.
- Round pre-walking implemented.
- Moves now have no fall recovery, like in the original game.
- Can now do standing grab while opponent is in the air and/or hurt, like in the original game.
- Moves have been given OTG properites.
- Needless juggle pointers have been removed.
- DP Super command altered to be more accurate.
- More effects from the game have been added.
- Likely a bunch of other stuff I forgot to mention.
