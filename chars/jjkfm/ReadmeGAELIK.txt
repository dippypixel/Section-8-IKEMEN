Kung Fu Man and Evil Kung Fu Man : Jojo's Bizarre Adventure Style !
Made by Gaelik
Original char by Elecbyte
JBA Sprites and SFX from Warusaki3's Dio and y.y's Joseph Joestar
Voice from Byakko's CVS Joe
Big portrait from BK's King Fancy Man (which gave me the idea o_O !)
Evil KFM 2009 stuff (for the stand) by The_None

Controls : Like Rubber Soul, Shadow Dio or Black Polnareff !
NOTE : These are the controls for the joystick.cmd, A1, A2 and A3 are x, y and z for the keyboard.cmd (and the b button is the shortcut of A1+A2+A3)
QCF, A1|A2|A3 : Kung Fu Palm
QCB, A1|A2|A3 : Kung Fu Upper
Fwd,Dwn,DwnFwd, A1|A2|A3 : Kung Fu Kick
QCF, A1+A2+A3 : Super KF Palm
QCB, A1+A2+A3 : Super KF Upper
QCB, Stand : Stand Combo
