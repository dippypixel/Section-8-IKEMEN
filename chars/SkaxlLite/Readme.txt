------------------------------
MOVELIST
------------------------------

Skaxl Roa plays like an Akatsuki Blitzkampf character. X, Y and Z are the three attack buttons, A is throw, B is counter, and C is the level 3 super.

===BASICS

Low Kick
	-	f, Y
	
Spin Kick
	-	f, Z
	
Slide Kick
	-	df, Z
	
Low Aireal Kick
	 -	d, Y (while in air)

	 
	 
===SPECIALS

Lets Just Throw Shit Alpha (Chintao's Speghetti Special)
	-	d, df, f, X
	
Lets Just Throw Shit Beta (Taokaka Is Too Tall)
	-	d, df, f, Y
	
Lets Just Throw Shit Gamma (Unleash The Tiger)
	-	d, df, f, Z
	
Chargin Alpha (Sexxy Sai)
	-	d, db, b, X
	
Chargin Beta (Tanjeh!?)
	-	d, db, b, Y
	
Chargin Gamma (Grandmother Crusher)
	-	d, db, b, Z
	
Still Not Slick
	-	d, d, X / Y / Z (can be done in air)

Woodbashing Extravaganza
	-	f, d, df, X / Y / Z
	
	
	
===SUPERS

Exprosive
	-	d, df, f, X+Y / Y+Z / X+Z
	
One In The Pipe
	-	d, df, f, X+Y / Y+Z / X+Z (while in the air)
	
Sabretooth's Political Commentary
	-	d, db, b, X+Y / Y+Z / X+Z
	
Slick Storm
	-	d, d, X+Y / Y+Z / X+Z
	
Grant Him Your Strength
	-	X+Y+Z, or C (Level 3 Super)
