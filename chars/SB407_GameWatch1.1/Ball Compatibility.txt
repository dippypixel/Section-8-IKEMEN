Mr. Game & Watch has a special compatibility standard for whenever he performs a throw.
Normally, he will toss the opponent around, but they are turned into their icons.

==Adding compatibility==

Step 1. Go to your preferred Fighter Factory and add a sprite in the sff of your character. Neither the index or group number matter.

Step 2. Make sure to align the sprite in the center. Do not align it via animations.

Step 3. Add the animation "1980685" and add the recently added sprite. The timing doesn't matter, so you can make it have more than 1 frame if you wish to.

And you're done!
