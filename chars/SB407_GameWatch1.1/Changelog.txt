==V1.1 (??/??/25)==
G&W has recieved some nerfs and bugfixes in this update. His combo game is hurt by the OTG IPS but it helps to prevent infinites and his overall damage output has been decreased a bit.
He's still a very potent fighter with strong damage output and combos, just to a lesser extent now.

*[General]       - A noticeable amount of quality-of-life changes to make him more in-line with my more recent characters
*[General]       - Removed pushbox helpers
*[Mechanics]	 - Can now recover by pressing c.
*[Mechanics]	 - Updated underground juggle and corner push codes from P.O.T.S' newest versions of their characters
*[Mechanics]	 - Roman Cancels have been slightly nerfed; using it decreases power gain by 80% instead of 75%.
*[Mechanics]     - Refined parrying a little bit
*[Mechanics]     - OTG IPS modified (to prevent infinites while still allowing for extensive combos)
*[Mechanics]     - Reduced default command time from 30 to 15
*[Visual]   	 - Added Dream Eater compatibility with my Gengar
*[Visual]   	 - More FX/SFX have been added all around to make G&W more visually striking.
*[Visual]        - Level 1 portrait is now assigned to palettes
*[Visual]        - Cleaned up Level 3 portrait
*[Bugfix]   	 - All OTG moves now have the same air.velocity as their down.velocity (to allow for more consistent OTG combos on M.U.G.E.N 1.0/1.1)
*[Bugfix]   	 - Judge's 8 no longer freezes the opponent after guarding the move in the air.
*[Bugfix]        - Fixed a bug that prevented the opponents from recovering out of moves with soft knockdown.
*[Bugfix]        - Fixed the opponent being thrown in the wrong direction when thrown by a corner on IKEMEN GO
*[Bugfix]	 - Fixed typo that caused freezing animation to not play when the opponent is hit by Judge's 8.
*[Bugfix/Buff]	 - Final hit on Turtle Bridge is now properly an overhead.
*[Buff/Nerf] 	 - Adjusted certain Judge attacks to vary the usage of the move.
*[Buff]    	 - Helmet now OTGs
*[Buff]    	 - Octopus deals everso slightly more damage
*[Buff]		 - Shortened how long dodges are to make it harder to punish with a throw.
*[Buff]		 - Each individual projectile spawned by Helmet does more damage and slightly more hitstun.
*[Nerf]     	 - Most attacks generally deal slightly less damage.
*[Nerf]      	 - Helmet was given significantly more startup time to hopefully make the move less frustrating to deal with
*[Nerf]		 - Helmet now spawns less projectiles (10 -> 5). While each projectile individually does more damage, its less effective as a combo tool.

==1.0b-f (09/25/22-10/06/22)==
Over a small list of updates, G&W received a mix of buffs, nerfs and bugfixes.

*[Mechanics]	 - You can now wavedash out of Super Jump's jumpsquat state.
*[Mechanics]	 - You can be thrown while in jumpsquat if you are in the middle of a wavedash.
*[Visual]	 - Fixed parry effect for crouching parries
*[Visual]	 - The opponent's shadow now disappears when under the ground while grabbed during Octopus.
*[Bugfix]	 - Fixed a crash relating to air dodges (thanks to XBrosion for letting me know)
*[Bugfix]	 - Fixed opponent being frozen anyway when guarding 8...on the ground at least.
*[Bugfix]	 - Opponent 'should' no longer be in freeze state twice.
*[Bugfix]	 - Helmet is now actually classified as a hyper projectile
*[Bugfix/Buff]	 - Judge's 8 now forces the opponent out of fall, which makes it more reliable for certain combos.
*[Bugfix/Nerf]	 - Throws now add to damage scaling
*[Buff]		 - Invincibility on the ground versions of Bombs Away are increased significantly
*[Buff]		 - Boxing now tracks the opponent, hopefully making the move hit more consistently.
*[Nerf]		 - Bombs Away now has more landing lag and deals significantly less hitstun on downed opponents (to prevent an infinite I found)
*[Nerf]		 - Vermin now has less hitstun on downed opponents (makes it harder to spam Judge after an OTG)
*[Nerf]		 - You can no longer Roman Cancel after landing Judge's 1.
