Mr. Game & Watch
Made by Somebody407 and matoh

==Gameplay==
Mr. Game & Watch is a 6-buttoned character who uses 4 buttons for attacks. Z is used to activate Roman Cancels, allowing Mr. Game & Watch to reset into his idle state during attacks. C is used for dodging.
Mr. Game & Watch utilizes many mechanics from different fighting games, with some examples being the Guilty Gear series and Super Smash Bros. Ultimate.

==Pros+Cons==
+ Diverse moveset, giving Mr. Game & Watch tons of options
+ Typically has great range on a lot of his attacks
+ Oil Panic completely nullifies projectile-based characters

= Very floaty (While he has a better aerial presence, he can also be punished easier while in the air.)
= Some attacks rely on RNG

- Extremely fragile (low health)
- Some moves have big hurtboxes to compensate for the large hitboxes 
- Outside of system mechanics, G&W has overall poor defense and loses to characters that can overwhelm him

==Mechanics==

Press F or B twice / x+y = Dash/Run
                    F or B when dashing - Dashdance


Tap F/D = Parry

z or 3 attack buttons = Roman Cancel

c = Dodge

D, U / U after using Launcher / a+b = Super Jump

any 2 buttons when falling = Recovery

==Normals==
x = Bell
y = Torch
a = Greenhouse
b = Lion

D+x = Hammer Sweep
D+y = Octopus Helmet (Launcher)
D+a = Manhole
D+b = Vermin (OTG)

x (in air) = Parachute
y (in air) = Turtle Bridge
a (in air) = Box
b (in air) = Donkey Kong Jr. (Knocks down aerial opponents)

==Command Normals==

F/B + y (when close) = Ball - G&W grabs an opponent and turns them into their icon. Direction the opponent is thrown depends on the direction you were holding.

F/B + b (when close) = Ball (Downwards) - Same concept as its normal variant, but G&W throws the opponent at an angle that allows for easier combos.
					  Does less damage, however.

a/b while dashing forward = Dash Attack - Performs a headbutt while wearing a helmet. Ok range, but G&W goes low profile during this move. Also an OTG.

DB, x (on ground) = Flagman - G&W swings a flag in front of him, and then a flag behind him. Can start combos, but generally isn't consistent for combos.
			      Good for crossing up opponents.

U, x (in air) = Tropical Fish - G&W pulls out a fishbowl above him and fish come out. While not the most powerful tool, it has absurdly good range and is good for juggling.

U, y (in air) = Spitball Sparky - Spits out a giant puff of air upward. An excellent anti-air move that gives G&W good juggles.
                                  However, it's usage is limited due to it having non-existant horizontal range.

==Specials==

QCF, x/y = Chef - Flips food out of a frying pan, which slowly descend. The trajectory of the food can be changed depending on the direction you hold.
                  The pan also has a hitbox, making the move very threatening in corners. You can repeat the attack 4 times by mashing the punch buttons.
                  The move can also potentially be a combo starter, albeit a very laggy and unreliable one. It's also worth noting that Chef is a good way for G&W to gain meter.
                  However, Mr. Game & Watch is left vulnerable after attacking, even moreso if the move is used multiple times.
                  The move is the same no matter what the strength of the attack is.

DP, a/b = Fire - Jumps off of a trampoline and slowly descends with a parachute. The parachute can be removed by holding down.
                 While not very powerful, Mr. Game & Watch is immune to all attacks during the majority of this move.
                 If whiffed or blocked, however, Mr. Game & Watch is often put in a bad position, making the move risky to use in neutral. It is also fairly hard to combo into, due to the angle that the move goes.
                 That being said, Fire is still a great anti-air and combo finisher due to its speed, distance, and invulnerability.
                 Very effective for starting air combos via Roman Cancels as well.
                 Ignores velocity deterioration, has overhead properties, and is a soft knockdown.
                 How far Mr. Game & Watch goes up, the parachute appearing and the damage output change depending on the strength of the attack.

                 
QCB, x/y = Judge - Swings a hammer in front of him, and holds up a flag with a number between 1 and 9.
AIR OK             This move can drastically change the game in favor of or against Mr. Game & Watch, depending on the number landed and the current situation you are in. Use at your own risk.
                   It should be noted that you can never get the same number twice in a row, to make the move more fair (so you don't end up getting a bunch of 9s or a bunch of 1s.)
                   The move is the same no matter what the strength of the attack is.

                         1 - An incredibly weak swing that barely flinches the opponent. Loses 100 health if used, and is easily punishable. You cannot Roman Cancel out of 1 as well, increasing the consequence for landing it.
                         2 - A weak swing that puts the opponent into a footsie.
                         3 - A weak swing that pulls the opponent to Mr. Game & Watch. 
                         4 - A swing that puts the opponent into soft knockdown.
                         5 - A swing with an electric effect. Also a multi-hit.
                         6 - A swing with a flame effect. Has the second-best frame advantage of all Judge attacks. Also puts opponent into soft knockdown when hit in the air.
                         7 - A swing with moderate damage and hitstun. Gives Mr. Game & Watch 67 health if it hits the opponent.
                         8 - A swing that does little damage, but freezes the opponent. Allows for easy followups.
                         9 - An extremely damaging swing that deals a lot of damage! It also puts the opponent in a hard knockdown.

QCB, a/b = Oil Panic - Holds out an oil drum, and absorbs any normal or special projectiles. The bucket can be filled with three weak projectiles, or 1 strong projectile.
AIR OK                 Once the bucket is full, the move turns into an attack. Mr. Game & Watch spills out a splash of oil from the bucket, with very good range.
                       This move is especially deadly as it nearly nullifies the playstyle of some characters, especially most projectile-based zoners.
                       The attack also comes out at frame 2, giving Mr. Game & Watch an extremely powerful tool that can be used as a fantastic combo finisher.
                       However, the move has few uses against characters who don't rely on projectiles and can often leave yourself vulnerable to melee attacks.
                       The attack's damage output varies depending on what projectiles are absorbed, but can only go up to 275 damage.
                       Bucket jumping is implemented in this iteration of the move.
		       Light version has more active frames, but longer recovery too. Heavy version has less recovery, but less active frames.

RDP, x/y = Bombs Away - Mr. Game & Watch jumps backwards and throws a bomb in front of him, which explodes after 30 ticks or when it hits the ground.
AIR OK                  The bomb itself can easily be destroyed by any attack, and doesn't explode upon hitting the opponent.
                        The explosion itself has fairly decent damage and range, however.
			The explosion also has OTG properties, allowing for Mr. Game & Watch to get combos off of it.
                        This move mainly functions as a mix-up/combo tool more than it does an actual approach tool.
                        How high up Mr. Game & Watch jumps changes depending on the strength of the attack.

==Supers==

QCF, x+y = Boxing (1 super bar) - Mr. Game & Watch turns into a boxer and punches toward the enemy.
                                  Due to its far range and having not a lot of startup, Boxing is arguably one of Mr. Game & Watch's best combo finishers.
				  First 3 hits are overheads, while the final hit is a low-hitting move.
                                  However, it does leave Mr. Game & Watch vulnerable if whiffed.
                                  Has startup invincibility.

QCB, a+b = Helmet (1 super bar) - A barrage of objects fall from the sky. Mr. Game & Watch can still move during this super and use all of his other attacks.
				  While not so strong on their own, the objects allow G&W do perform long combo strings.
                                  The objects can still be hit by other projectiles.

HCF, a+b = Octopus (3 super bars) - Mr. Game & Watch's Final Smash. He turns into a giant octopus and floats forwards, grabbing the opponent with his tentacles.
AIR OK                              Comes out extremely fast and covers about the whole screen.
				    Holding up and down will make G&W move in that direction.
				    Not affected by damage scaling.
