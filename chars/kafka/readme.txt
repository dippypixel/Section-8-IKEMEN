Kaf Ka (or Kap Ka if you prefer) from Battle Monsters by The_None

Kaf Ka looks to be a deformed, evil witch doctor of sorts, but this is merely a deceptive look. He is a man of simple pleasures:
he comes from a long bloodline of a tribe known as the tricksters. Shortly after his birth, he indulged in his own brand of magical powers.
His only purpose of this power was to cause mischief. As he reached his forties (very old in trickster years), he became very efficient at
all sorts of magic, but his motives haven't changed in the least. To others he may appear menacing or confrontional, and he can be. But
that's only if you get in the way of his motives. Those motives being just generally fucking around with people. Before he passes on, he
has added only one goal to his bucket list: to cause minor inconvienence to everyone.

Note:This char is not suited for simul mode
