ZMR Character Template for Zander's Mugen Remastered (Demo):
Game and Template by Alexander Cooper 19, assistance on effects and Corndog Orbs by Viola.
extra files for help with custom hyper move KO messages and in-game lifebar icons are found in "Pixlr Files" folder.

NOTE: in normal mugen/Ikemen Go only you can ignore these steps, but if you are making a character for Zander's Mugen Remastered its adviced you
read in on the rules so it can fit in with the game and its mechanics.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if you are confused on any of the mechanics, notes are spread around the characters CNS files to look at to get a better understanding of the game
and what mechanics would be included.

1. keep the following mechanics from the template, which include these. if none of these are here then its not a ZMR character. 

|| Mandatory Requirements
- three 1 bar hypers each and 1 full bar of meter per character and no more than 3 hypers.
- three special moves (all with light, medium and heavy variants) no more than 3 special moves.
- one default grounded grab (with it being able to be teched), air default grabs are forbidden.
- 60 tick getup time and air juggle gravity system (12 juggle points system of mugen if not against ZMR Characters)
- Parries/White Parries system, 1 Dodge and Short Hops
- Universal Launcher (statedef 420)
- Aerial Rave hard knockdowns (with Custom States)
- Corndog Orbs and Tag Team mechanics (they're built into the characters)
- air guarding turned off, and guard crush state for situational use cases.
- custom knockdown state (5111) that prevents instant get ups from the original engine.
- a taunt, and taunt cancels (via specials and hypers from taunt)
- fall.defense.up completely turned off (set to 0 for consistency)
- damage scaling system and any UI elements like like "KO", "YOU WIN!", "First Attack", "Counter", "Aerial Rave" and Hyper Combo Finishes
- the ability to turn mid jump arc if over an opponent
- gravitiy based juggle system (and points based juggle system normal engine uses)
- 12 color palettes minimum

|| Optional Requirements
- Custom Character Art drawn in games portrait artstyle
- ZMR Portraits for versus screen and lifebars ("9000,6968" and "9000,6969" in SFF)
- ZMR Character detector 'managed by Var(21)'
- 13 extra color palettes (25 colors in total)
- Blitz Mode Compatibility (code found in TagTeamZMR.cns)

2. keep them using the 3 button XYZ layout (X, Y and Z for your attacks, with A being taunt, B to switch partner in tag team mode, C to summon partner in tag team mode) to be consistent with the other characters.

3. if you're designing a character for ZMR, make things fair. for regular Mugen/Ikemen Go do what you like, but for ZMR this is an order. if the characters officially made for the game and it
ends up being to powerful then balance adjustments will be put to practice by the director.

4. any moves that heal are banned. characters can also have their own mechanics but nothing to overpowered like Stands in JJBA HFTF as thats also banned.

5. do NOT create Strikers/Assists in any form shape or compacity if you are making a character for ZMR with this template. Corndog Orbs and Tag Team team modes already fill that role of assist in the game.

6. don't replace the previously existing hitsparks or hitsounds, doing that is a red flag.

7. make sure to make brand new palettes and modify the default to have slightly different shadings to make them more unique from other character ports for the game. each character has 12 palettes minimum
and 25 max in ZMR that can be choosen via palette selector.

8. don't use the Projectile controller for any Projectile as stupid as that sounds. instead Projectiles should be Helpers, this way you get much more flexability with what you can do with them.
   Kung Fu Man's Planks can be used as a template for your own projectiles, as they are coded with a helper.

9. when modifying CPU, make sure to take into account that the CPU itself can be influenced by the difficulty toggle in the engine's settings, and for experienced programmers don't try to make it
   to strong when developing for ZMR, as it wouldn't be very fun to fight against.

10. whenever possible, make the characters speak english only. this is consistent across the whole cast for english speakers have a easier time with understanding voice clips on all characters.
    mainly due to fact ZMR is an english only game in terms of languages.

11. depending on your character's size, remember to adjust the hitbox/hurtbox of your characters Corndog Orb. anim number in .AIR is "10000" or named "Orb Hitbox"
