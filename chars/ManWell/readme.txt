This is where everything you need to know is, Including Movesets & Credits.

-[Defining some phrases used]-


QCF- Down, Down-Forward, Forward (or just Hitting Down, Pressing Forward then let go of Down)

QCB- Down, Down-Back, Back (Same as above but opposite)

 + - Press at the same time (EX: X+Y = X and Y at the same time)


-[Moveset]-

Misc:


D, Up- Super Jump (Hold Left/Right to slowly alter direction)

Z/C- Dodge (Hold Left/Right to travel further, can be done in mid-air)

Forward, Forward- Run forward

Back, Back- Run Backward


Basics:


X- Light Punch

Y- Strong Punch

A- Light Kick

B- Strong Kick

X+Y/A+B- Grab


Specials:


QCF, X/QCF, Y- Plastic Bat (Uses a baseball bat to knock out the opponent when close enough, X stays stationary while Y makes him slide forward quite a bit, Can be done mid-air and may travel further mid air)

QCF, A/QCF, B- Furious Swipes (Lunges and Constantly throws essentially every normal he has, X Lunges forward while Y makes him go up, can be done mid-air)

QCB, X/QCB, Y- Fireball (Throws a limited range fireball, X throws shorter while Y throws further, depending on your power he may throw a big multi hit dorito, can be done mid-air)

QCB, A/QCB, B- Foot Lettuce (Man U Well floats above the player wearing bins of lettuce and decends onto the player, A and B have no difference, can be done mid-air)
