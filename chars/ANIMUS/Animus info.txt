ANIMUS

- version 3.0-
(20/1/2023)

Author: Andres borghi


Animus has a customizable fatal move. You can adapt your char to be killed by Animus using this tutorial:

https://web.archive.org/web/20170508134047/http://www.andresborghi.com/mugen/CUSTOMIZE.htm


This char is part of a full game called The Black Heart. It's the first mugen/ikemen game to be on STEAM. In the full game you can see the intros and endings for the chars and experience the complete story.
Get it here:

https://store.steampowered.com/app/1431780/The_Black_Heart/
