* The power of fluffy boys, mean girls, and eating moss shines within you.

Key: Deltarune Trio only use these buttons:
F = Forward
B = Back
U = Up
D = Down  (Normal config for these applies to arrow keys)
x = X     (Kris attacks, normal config is L )
y = Y     (Commands Ralsei's attacks, normal config is ; )
z = Z     (Commands Susie's attacks, normal config is " )
S = Start (Taunt, normal config is Enter )

 Ex.) DF = Down + Forward


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Technical Properties:

Passive (  )
~ Party System
Deltarune Trio is made up of three different characters. You control Kris directly, while Susie and Ralsei will
follow orders and stand close to Kris. As the leader, Kris commands Ralsei with Y and Susie with Z. Both Party
Members have a HP bar in the corner of the screen, indicating how much health they have left. Ralsei and Susie's
health is separate from Kris' health, and the fight is only won if Kris is defeated, as they are the leader. 
Should either Ralsei or Susie be KO'd, they can no longer be commanded to attack until the next round.
- Kris acts as the main fighter, and all party members follow behind him.
- Ralsei will always follow closely behind Kris... A bit clingy, that one.
- Susie will stay and protect Ralsei while in a vulnerable state, such as using Lullaby or when he is out of HP.

( S )
~ Taunt
Spin in a pirouette to Heal your other party members slightly (not including Kris) and charge Power.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Specials:  

(D, DF, F, x)
~ SOUL Rebound
A reflect/counter used by Kris, bouncing enemies back with the culmination of your being. Counter only applies
while Kris is facing the screen and a projectile/attack connects.

(D, DF, F, y)
~ Lullaby
Ralsei sings a soft, sweet melody to lull the enemy to sleep. If he is not interrupted, the opponent will snooze
peacefully for a period of time, leaving them wide open. Does not effect snoozing or tripped enemies.

(D, DF, F, z)
- Rude Buster
A blast of Rude Magic is launched by Susie, dealing large projectile damage. The attack is hefty, but has a bit
of startup time and can be blocked.

(D, DB, B, x)
~ Assist: Lancer
Ho Ho Ho! Lancer rides in behind the opponent, crashing into them and sending them flying. Can be blocked.

(D, DB, B, y)
~ Assist: Rouxls Kaard
Cowerth, thine worms! A large puzzle box is dropped at the opponent's current location to deal damage.

(D, DB, B, z)
~ Assist: Jevil
Despite being able to DO ANYTHING, Jevil will teleport around rapidly and release a triple shot of magic bullets
each time before warping away with a small quip. 

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hypers:
(These moves are akin to TP Moves from the game, and as such will heal Susie and Ralsei for a small amount.)

(D, D, x+y) [Costs 1000 power] 
~ Friendly Fire
Susie tosses Ralsei at the opponent, launching them with moderate damage and knockback. 

(D, D, x+z) [Costs 1500 power]
~ SPARE
If the opponent's HP is low enough and enough time has passed, you can SPARE them to instantly win the fight!
The enemy isn't defeated, so they'll also celebrate alongside you using their Victory Animations.

(D, D, y+z) [Costs 2000 power] 
~ All-Out Bout
Here comes the pain! All party members attack the opponent at once, ending with a massive explosion for huge
damage and high knockback.
