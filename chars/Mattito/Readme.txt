Mattito version 0.8


[readme made in 2026]


This character is blatantly just not finished. After over five years making an attempt at my own OC (2012-2018) I
ultimately failed to do so. This iteration was made for a game that never got far so there's not a whole lot of
"funny" in here. I don't think this would have lived up to the legacy of my friends' OC releases but I always
desperately wanted to fit in so in the end I guess I'm partially glad I at least made a playable version of him.

The character's moveset is pretty basic and barebones, I had ideas that never happened. I just don't care at all
about this guy anymore, I don't even think I've drawn him since 2018.

And as much as I know people love to use characters as templates for their own I'm going to say right now that
while I don't think anyone would do that for "Mattito", I would respect you a lot more if you made your own
character designs. It took me 6 years to get to the point where I could do that. It doesn't matter if it looks
like and plays like shit, just keep making things you want to make and you will continue to improve.


--------------------------Fundamental Shit--------------------------

s. = stand
c. = crouch
a. = air

LK - a
HK - b
LP - x
HP - y

grab - c


--------------------------Command Normals--------------------------

Bitch Slap (overhead) - F + s.HP

Dick Kick - F + s.LK

--------------------------Special Moves--------------------------

Baseball - QCF,  x/y (ground)
- Throws a baseball underhanded at the opponent
   - "x" version travels in an arc and hits opponent upward
   - "y" version travels slightly upward and hits opponent away

Bat Attack idfk - QCB,  x/y (ground/air)
- Swings a baseball bat at the opponent. Has pretty high lag but fast startup.
   - grounded "x" version hits opponent up and backwards, but airborne opponents away
   - grounded "y" version hits opponent away
   - aerial "x" version hits grounded opponents up, but airborn opponents somewhat downward
   - aerial "y" version hits grounded opponents away, but airborn opponents up and backward
   - aerial "x" version can combo into the "y" version

Slide - F, F, a/b
- Slides across the screen (the graphics were temporary but I never made real ones)
   - "y" version travels noticeably farther than the "x" version but I think both function the same

--------------------------Super Moves--------------------------

Fireball (level 1) - QCF x2, x/y
- Throws a flaming baseball underhanded at the opponent that hits multiple times

Bat Tornado (level 1) - QCB x2, x/y (ground/air)
- Spins a bat around several times before a final blow. You can move forward and backwards freely and it hits on both sides
- This move stands the opponent when it hits so it always combos properly.
- Can be cancelled into Fireball

Shoe Gun (level 3) - QCF x2, x+y (ground)
- shoots the opponent with a very strangely shaped gun
