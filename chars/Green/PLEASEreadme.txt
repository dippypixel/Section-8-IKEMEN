Green, but actually finished! I will be updating this character accordingly from now on, and I will put the version and the changelogs in this readme.txt
The stick figure fighting for fun and memes, a six button character who's combos are a mashup of MVC and the other chars I have played in M.U.G.E.N.
He has a lot of moves in his disposal, from inside jokes, to references to games, etc! Basically, expect anything when you fight him for the first time!
His AI is a bit brutal, but is even worse if you disable the nerf for the assists in the config. There is no AI scaling, sorry.
The readme will be refering to the move commands as what the commands are in the cmd file.
YOU ALSO DO NOT NEED PERMISSION TO EDIT MY CHARACTERS OR GIVE AI PATCHES, JUST NO NSFW OR VORE AND WE WILL BE GOOD! :D
