Thank you for your download! "Shuma-Gorath" is a character from Marvel Comics, cosmic
enemy of Dr. Strange. This character was developed based on the Versus Series Game System,
inspired by the Marvel Vs. Capcom franchise.

Vitality: 					950
Chain Combo Archetype: 				Hunter Series

Movelist:

+ System
  - Dash [in air OK]				F, F / PP / F + PP
  - Back Dash [in air OK]			B, B / B + PP
  - Super Jump					D, U / KK
  - Counter					HP + HK (only blocking)
  - Taunt					Start

+ Command Attacks:
  - Air Strange Gaze [air only]			U + HP
  - Air Drop [air only]				D + HK

+ Throws:
  - Bite Throw					F + HP / B + HP
  - Life Steal					F + HK / B + HK
	Can steal up to 50 HP from enemy

+ Special Attack:
  - Mystic Smash [in air OK]			D, DF, F + K
  - Mystic Stare 				CHARGE B, F + P
  - Devitalization 				F, DF, D, DB, B + K
  - Mystic Ray	 				CHARGE D, U + P

+ Hyper Attacks:
  - Hyper Mystic Smash (LVL 1)			D, DF, F + KK
  - Hyper Mystic Ray (LVL 1)			D, DB, B + PP
  - Chaos Dimension (LVL 3)			D, DF, F + PP
    + Chaos Dimension Attack [in air OK]	LP + LK
