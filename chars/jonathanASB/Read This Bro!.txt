Jonathan Joestar By Amarimono edited by QuickFist

This is an extensive edit of Amarimono's Jonathan. 
He has a custom gameplay inspired by BlazBlue Cross Tag Battle and Fantasy Strike.

3 attack buttons:
X - Light
Y - Medium
Z - Heavy

3 system buttons:
A - Hamon Breathing (Power charge)
B - Evade
C - Throw

Specials:
Forward X: OverDrive Rendan
Forward Y: Hamon Wave 
Back Y: Hamon Wave reverse
Forward Z: Scarlet OverDrive
Back Z: Pluck Sword Triple Slash

Supers:
Lvl1 - QCF, C: Sendo Hamon 
Lvl2 - QCB, C: Sunlight Yellow OverDrive
SDM (Only usable with 2 bars and in a third of life) - B+C: The Last Hamon
